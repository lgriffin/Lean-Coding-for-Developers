Subject: Staging deploy script: 1 week of data

I've been using a script to automate staging deploys this week.
Here's what I measured:

  Before (manual):  avg 17 min/deploy, 4 deploys/week = 68 min/week
  After (script):   avg 3 min/deploy,  4 deploys/week = 12 min/week
  Saved:            56 min/week across my deploys alone

The script handles image pull, config update, migration,
restart, and Slack notification. Log tailing is still manual.

It's in /tools/deploy-staging.sh if anyone wants to try it.
No pressure, just sharing what worked for me.
